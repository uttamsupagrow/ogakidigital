(ABOUT US)

 Made For Prestige Beauty

OGAKI was founded with a clear mission: to build an agency for prestige beauty that empowers brands to thrive in a social-first world.

We’re not an influencer-only shop or a full-service machine. We’re a team of beauty specialists and social-first experts - deeply invested in the work and the brands behind it. Strategic partners and creative visionaries who understand that how it looks and how it performs are inseparable.

For us, social-first is more than an approach - it’s the foundation of growth for modern prestige beauty.
