COMMUNICATIONS THAT POWER GROWTH

At OGAKI, we know that communications goes well beyond traditional press mentions. SEO is supported by the link-building and brand mentions created by media relations. Publications utilize affiliate marketing in their shopping stories, gift guides, and more. Retailers look for brand buzz in the beauty industry. And PR generates the awareness, credibility, and leads necessary to drive sales and retention.

By leveraging our unique expertise across marketing and communications, we build stronger media relations programs that not only garner more impressions with your target audience, but also help optimize all of your other marketing efforts.

SPECIALIZING IN ELEVATED BEAUTY

Our dedicated beauty experts have decades of experience in media relations working with prestige beauty brands. We leverage our longstanding editor relationships, deep beauty knowledge, and expertise in affiliate marketing to garner coverage in the key publications that reach your target audience.

We know exactly how to pitch a beauty editor versus a commerce editor, what pitches to send when, what trend the industry is about to cover, and how to clearly and concisely break down science-driven messaging. Our approach maximizes coverage in both traffic-driving and credibility-building publications.
